# 08 - Research and Hacking

## Goal

Turn papers, ideas, and rough system intuitions into measured prototypes
without losing the discipline needed to learn from them.

## Build

- A paper reproduction plan.
- A minimal baseline.
- One ablation at a time.
- A result log that records what changed, what happened, and what remains uncertain.

## Measure

- The metric claimed by the original idea.
- At least one systems metric: latency, memory, throughput, utilization, or cost.
- Failure cases and sensitivity to assumptions.

## Teach Back

Explain what the experiment actually showed, what it did not show, and
what evidence would change the conclusion.

## Stretch

Convert one promising prototype into a reusable module, benchmark, or
teaching lab elsewhere in the repo.
